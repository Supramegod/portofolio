# Backlink Opportunity Finder

Use `websearch` to research a domain or niche, then generate:

1. **Competitor backlinks** — find sites linking to competitors (search: `link:competitor.com` or `"competitor" "resources"`).
2. **Broken link targets** — find dead resources pages in the niche that could be replaced.
3. **Guest post opportunities** — blogs that accept guest writers in the same niche.
4. **Directory/list pages** — curated lists that are open for submissions.
5. **Resource pages** — pages that link out to tools, articles, or references.

For each opportunity, list:
- URL
- Domain Authority (estimated: high/medium/low)
- Relevance (match to user's niche)
- Outreach difficulty (hard/medium/easy)
