# LSI Keyword Generator

Given a seed keyword:

1. Brainstorm 8-15 semantically related terms and phrases.
2. Categorize them:
   - **Synonyms** — direct alternatives
   - **Related concepts** — broader topics
   - **Long-tail variations** — question-based or 3-4 word phrases
   - **Entity-based** — brands, tools, people associated
3. For each term, note search intent: informational, navigational, commercial, transactional.

Output a table: Keyword | Category | Search Intent
