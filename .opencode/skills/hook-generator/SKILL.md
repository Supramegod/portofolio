# Hook Generator

Given a topic or product, generate 5 hook options:

## 5 Hook Formulas

| # | Type | Formula | Contoh |
|---|------|---------|--------|
| 1 | **Curiosity gap** | "Most people don't know [surprising fact]" | "Most devs skip this one deployment step" |
| 2 | **Problem-solution** | "Struggling with [problem]? Try [solution]." | "Struggling with slow APIs? Try Laravel Octane." |
| 3 | **Stat/Shock** | "[Number]% of [audience] [do something]." | "90% of portfolio sites fail the 6-second test." |
| 4 | **Story lead** | "I [did something] so you don't have to." | "I rebuilt my portfolio 3 times. Here's what works." |
| 5 | **Direct address** | "If you're a [target], stop scrolling." | "If you're a backend dev, this is for you." |

## Platform Fit

| Platform | Max Hook Words | Style |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| TikTok | < 10 words | Fast, trend-aware, musical |
| Instagram Reels | < 15 words | Aesthetic, storytelling |
| LinkedIn | < 40 words | Professional, value-driven |

For each hook, also note the emotional trigger (curiosity, fear, greed, vanity, outrage) and predicted engagement hook rate (high/medium/low).
