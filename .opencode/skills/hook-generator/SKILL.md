# Hook Generator

Given a topic or product, generate 5 hook options:

1. **Curiosity gap** — "Most people don't know this one trick..."
2. **Problem-solution** — "Struggling with [problem]? Try this."
3. **Stat/Shock** — "90% of marketers skip this step."
4. **Story lead** — "I tried 10 tools so you don't have to."
5. **Direct address** — "If you're a [target], stop scrolling."

For each hook, note:
- Platform fit (TikTok / Instagram / LinkedIn / all)
- Emotional trigger (curiosity, fear, greed, vanity, outrage)
- Predicted engagement hook rate (high/medium/low)

Prioritize short hooks (<15 words for TikTok/IG, <40 for LinkedIn).
