# CTR Optimizer

Given a topic and target audience, generate:

1. **Headline Variant A** — Benefit-driven ("Double Your Traffic in 30 Days")
2. **Headline Variant B** — Curiosity-driven ("The Strategy 99% of Marketers Miss")
3. **Headline Variant C** — Urgency-driven ("Last Chance: Free SEO Audit")
4. **Headline Variant D** — How-to driven ("How I Got 10K Visitors in a Week")
5. **Headline Variant E** — Social-proof driven ("Join 5,000+ Happy Users")

For each variant, provide:
- Character count (target: 30-55 for Google Ads headlines)
- Emotional angle
- Predicted CTR (low/medium/high)

Best practices: use numbers, power words, brackets for dynamic insertion.
