# Outreach Email Template

Generate a customizable email with these sections:

1. **Subject line** — personalized, ≤50 chars, includes recipient's site name.
2. **Greeting** — personal (use name, not "Dear Webmaster").
3. **Introduction** — who you are + genuine compliment about their content.
4. **Value proposition** — what you offer (guest post, resource, collaboration).
5. **Specific ask** — clear, one request only.
6. **Reciprocity** — what they gain (quality content, link, exposure).
7. **Closing** — polite, with full name + signature.

Include placeholders in `[brackets]` for:
- `[Recipient Name]`
- `[Your Name]`
- `[Your Site URL]`
- `[Topic/Offer]`
- `[Mutual Benefit]`

Tone options: professional, friendly, or direct. Default: professional.
